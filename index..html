About ARC

Making research accessible, practical and impactful

Adani Research & Consultancy (ARC) is a research and consultancy initiative committed to advancing knowledge, supporting academic development, and providing evidence-informed insights for better decision-making.


Based in Hargeisa, Somaliland, ARC seeks to bridge the gap between research and practice by helping students, researchers, institutions, and professionals turn ideas into meaningful outcomes. Our work focuses on research support, data analysis, academic development, economic research, and the responsible use of artificial intelligence in research and education.


We believe that reliable evidence, analytical thinking, and accessible knowledge are essential for addressing complex challenges and supporting sustainable development.

Our Mission

To support research, strengthen academic and analytical capacity, and provide evidence-informed consultancy that helps individuals and institutions make informed decisions and address practical challenges.

Our Vision

To become a trusted research and consultancy institution recognised for academic integrity, analytical quality, innovation, and meaningful contributions to knowledge and development in Somaliland and beyond.

What we do

Our Services

01

Research and Academic Support

Guidance on research topic development, research questions, proposal preparation, literature reviews, research design, and methodology. Our goal is to help students and researchers develop stronger research skills and understand the principles of rigorous academic work.

02

Data Collection and Analysis

Support for planning data collection, questionnaire development, data organisation, and the interpretation of quantitative and qualitative findings. Where appropriate, we provide guidance on analytical tools such as SPSS and Jamovi.

03

Economic and Policy Research

We explore economic and social issues through research, data interpretation, and evidence-informed analysis, including inflation, employment, household welfare, economic development, livelihoods, and public policy in Somaliland and the wider region.

04

AI in Research and Education

We promote the responsible use of AI tools to support literature discovery, research organisation, academic learning, and productivity, emphasising verification, critical evaluation, academic integrity, and human judgement.

05

Academic Development and Mentorship

We support students and emerging researchers in developing research literacy, academic writing skills, analytical thinking, and confidence throughout the research process.

06

Consultancy and Project Support

Research-informed analysis, project planning, problem assessment, and practical recommendations tailored to the needs of organisations, institutions, and professionals.

How we work

Our Research Approach

Meaningful research requires more than collecting information. It requires asking relevant questions, selecting appropriate methods, examining evidence critically, and communicating findings responsibly.

Identify the problemUnderstand the issue and the questions that need to be answered.
Review the evidenceExamine relevant literature, data, and credible sources.
Apply appropriate methodsSelect suitable research and analytical techniques.
Interpret the findingsExplain what the evidence shows, including its limitations.
Communicate practical insightsPresent findings clearly to support learning and decision-making.

We aim to distinguish verified findings from assumptions and opinions, ensuring that research communication remains transparent and responsible.

Knowledge sharing

ARC Research Insights

ARC Research Insights is our knowledge-sharing series dedicated to exploring research methods, academic development, economic issues, and emerging trends. Through concise research-based articles and analytical insights, we aim to make complex topics easier to understand and encourage informed discussion.

Economic trends and household welfare in Somaliland Employment, skills, and youth development Research methodology and data analysis Academic writing and research tools Artificial intelligence in research and education Evidence-based decision-making and public policy

Each insight should draw on credible sources wherever possible and clearly acknowledge the limitations of the available evidence.
